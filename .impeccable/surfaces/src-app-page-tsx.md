# Homepage (/)

Scope: the homepage of blueport.studio. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: small-business owners comparing studios; they need price, time and proof fast.
Action: Umów rozmowę (/kontakt); second: Zobacz ofertę (/oferta).
Proof on hand: six real client sites (public/work), three named testimonials, 10+ deployments, 24 h reply.
Constraints: keep every existing section and its copy intent (hero, client domains, three recent works, plan statement, three problems, pricing, process, testimonials, closing CTA, footer). Polish. Brand kit in design/logo/kit. Binding reference: payloadcms.com.
Prototype: design/site-v2/index.html (static, before the Next.js port).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page stands on its own visible construction grid, the same scaffolding the bp mark is cut from; price and time are shown as data, client sites as working screens. It refuses the gradient hero over a row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Tusz ground ruled by full-height 1px Grafit column lines (four columns plus margins at desktop, two on phones); a soft Kobalt light beam crossing the hero diagonally; Papier text; Sygnał only on the primary action and the logo's half disc. Geist at 500 with tight tracking for headlines, Geist Mono only for data (prices, days, domains). Zero radius. Actions are full-width hairline rows sitting in a grid column. Client sites live in dark browser windows that overlap.

STORY: In one screen the visitor reads what is sold and from what price, sees real sites being built; further down, proof and process; the close repeats the one action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: h1 over three lines, one lead sentence, two action rows (Umów rozmowę in Sygnał, Zobacz ofertę). Right three columns: a single browser window in which each real client site builds on the construction dots and then wipes in, in turn.

FORM: pinned by the owner in chat (payloadcms.com), position 1 of 1; concept-seed not run because new-work rules that a user-pinned direction beats the roll. Signature interaction: the hero build frame (a client site drawn on the grid, then wiped in); second: the works list swapping the browser window with a wipe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
