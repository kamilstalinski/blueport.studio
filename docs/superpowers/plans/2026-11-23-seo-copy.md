# SEO Copy Rewrite Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rewrite the copy and metadata of every content page of blueport.studio for SEO, with no price amounts or price-list references, keeping /cennik, /kalkulator, /wycena and the legal pages unchanged.

**Architecture:** Text-only changes in page `metadata`, section components and `src/constants/*`, guarded by two new tests: a vitest scan of copy sources for forbidden price wording, and a Playwright check of title/description length and H1 per route. One small data addition: an `seo` field per case study.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js App Router (TypeScript), vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-seo-copy-design.md`. It holds the keyword map, the voice rules and the price-mention rules. Every copy task writes from it.

## Global Constraints

- Polish, "Ty" form, plain and concrete, numbers instead of adjectives (PRODUCT.md voice).
- Only facts from PRODUCT.md and existing constants: 10+ wdrożeń, 1–2 tygodnie do publikacji od akceptacji projektu, odpowiedź do 24 h w dni robocze, Szczecin + praca zdalna, Kamil i Rafał, the eight client sites and their case studies. No invented clients, figures or quotes.
- No `zł`, no amounts, no "cennik", no "ile kosztuje", no "koszt" in user-facing copy in scope. Exception: "ceny" as something the client edits in their own panel. The calculator stays the primary CTA, labelled without cost words.
- Keyword map per page: the spec's "Mapa fraz" table. The main phrase goes in title, H1, the first paragraph and one or two H2s, inflected naturally.
- Title ≤ 60 characters including the template suffix " | Blueport Studio". Description 140–160 characters. Every title is unique.
- Headings describe the section or ask a real client question. No wordplay. FAQ and /proces put the answer first (the first 1–2 sentences answer directly).
- No AI tells: "to nie X, to Y", "bez X, bez Y, bez Z", "kompleksowe/innowacyjne/profesjonalne".
- Paragraph length stays close to today's, because the layout is fitted to it. No style or layout changes.
- Testimonial quotes (`testimonials.ts`), Navbar, Footer, HomePricing, TierCard, /cennik, /kalkulator, /wycena, /regulamin and /polityka-prywatnosci are not edited. The shared `CtaBand` changes everywhere, including /cennik.

## Review Focus

1. Phone width (375 px): longer Polish headings must not overflow or break the hero/PageHead layout. Each task's final step checks its pages at the mobile preset.
2. Shared components (`CtaBand`, `PageHead`, `QuotesSection`) render on excluded pages. A rewrite must read correctly there too, /cennik in particular.
3. Case-study titles double the brand today (`— Realizacja Blueport Studio | Blueport Studio`). The meta e2e test in Task 1 asserts that the suffix appears exactly once.
4. FAQ JSON-LD must keep matching the visible FAQ after the `price` item is removed. Task 6 asserts the JSON-LD question count equals the rendered count.
5. Price wording hiding in alts and `aria-label`s, not only visible text. The Task 1 vitest scan covers whole source files, attributes included.
